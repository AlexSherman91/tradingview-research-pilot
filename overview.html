How the research notebook works

PILOT CONCEPT · NOT THE CURRENT TRADINGVIEW UI

One company, one question: “How sustainable is its growth?” Move from a research goal to your own evidence-based conclusion.

The pilot adds a research plan, a structure for evidence and a final conclusion. It reuses existing financial data, filings and charts. AI is not included in the first pilot. Editing and saving data are not available.
